Simplex is a simple word processor.

One window, one document. It opens and saves Word files, prints to PDF, and stays on your own computer.

What it does

The things most documents need, and not much else.

Simple

No ribbon, no side panels. A toolbar and your document.

Works with Word

Opens and saves .docx. Send files to Word users as usual.

Small

A 2.6 MB download that starts instantly. Word needs a few gigabytes.

The basics

Tables, images, links, find and replace, spell check, printing.

Autosave

Saves while you type and keeps older versions. Your text survives a crash.

Inspection mode

Shows formatting you didn't mean to apply.

What it doesn't do

Windows only — on a Mac, use the browser version (tested in Chrome, Edge and Firefox; Safari isn't). Heavily formatted Word documents arrive simplified, though never with text missing. No collaboration, no comments, no track changes, no footnotes.

Simplex doesn't send anything.

No account, no telemetry, no ads. Your documents sit on your own disk as readable text you can open in any editor. This page loads nothing from anywhere else either — no fonts, no trackers, no cookies.

The source code is on GitHub if you want to check.
